Evaluation

An engine claims it can write in a style; a lecture claims a move works. Both claims have to be tested against results. Evaluation has three layers: daily marking, a machine screen, and human blind review.

01 · DAILY

Marking desk

Every day the writer side produces one piece with one engine. Drafts that fail the machine check go back for a rewrite; drafts that pass go to a reviewer, who checks the style points one by one, picks the single sentence most in need of revision, and draws one lesson. Everything is archived, rejected drafts included.

02 · MACHINE

AI-flavor checker

Scans for stock phrases, aphorism tone, stacked parallelism and internet slang, and scores each piece against its own engine’s standard. The score is a threshold, not a verdict: passing only means no obvious machine flavor. Whether it is good still takes a human reader.

03 · HUMAN

Blind review

For the same prompt, the engine version and the bare-model version are shuffled and handed to graders who are not told which is which, two graders per prompt. Win rates are judged with Wilson intervals; with too few samples, no conclusion is drawn.
