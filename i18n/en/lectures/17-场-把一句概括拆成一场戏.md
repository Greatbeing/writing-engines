# Lecture 17 · The Scene: Break a Summary into a Scene

"Those years were hard for our family." "He is extremely conscientious about his work." Sentences like these are summaries. A summary saves words, but when the reader finishes it they have seen nothing.

## 1. The one move to learn in this lecture

> **Pick the most important summary sentence in the piece and break it into a scene: one time, one place, at least two people, one action, one line of speech.**

The test: **once the scene is written, the summary can be deleted, and the reader still knows what it meant.**

## 2. How the masters do it

Zhu Ziqing sums up his father's situation in a single sentence:

> 那年冬天，祖母死了，父亲的差使也交卸了，正是祸不单行的日子。

*Translation:* That winter my grandmother died and my father lost his post. Misfortunes never come singly.

Right after it comes a scene:

> 到徐州见着父亲，看见满院狼藉的东西，又想起祖母，不禁簌簌地流下眼泪。父亲说，「事已如此，不必难过，好在天无绝人之路！」

*Translation:* When I met my father in Xuzhou and saw the courtyard strewn with our things, I thought of my grandmother again and the tears ran down. Father said, "What's done is done. No use grieving. Heaven always leaves a way out!"

A courtyard strewn with things, a son in tears, a father who comforts the son instead of the other way round, one line. The family's decline is right there in that courtyard.

**A summary tells the reader what happened; a scene puts the reader there.**

## 3. What AI writes

> During that time, our family went through many hardships, but we supported one another and eventually pulled through.

One sentence covers several years. The reader doesn't know which year it was, what the hardship was, or who supported whom.

## 4. One revision

**Original**
> He is extremely conscientious about his work.

**Breakdown**: "conscientious" is a verdict. There is no scene.

**Revised**
> Nine o'clock on New Year's Eve, the server room alarm went off. He took the call at the dinner table, put a piece of fish in his mouth with his chopsticks, picked up his coat and went out. His mother carried the plate of fish back to the wok to keep it warm. She warmed it three times.

Time: nine on New Year's Eve. Place: the dinner table. Two people: him and his mother. Actions: eating the fish, going out, warming the fish. Not one "conscientious".

## 5. Your exercise

Pick one of the three sentences below and break it into a scene, in under 170 words (the Chinese original sets 250 characters; 170 English words is roughly the same length):

- "Our homeroom teacher was very strict."
- "That summer was especially hot."
- "My dad and I have never really got along."

**Requirements:** include a time, a place, two people, an action and a line someone actually says. When you're done, delete the original sentence and see whether the scene still stands.
