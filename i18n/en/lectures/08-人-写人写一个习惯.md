# Lecture 8 · People: Write a Person Through One Habit

When beginners write about a person, they list the résumé: she was hardworking, kind, and spent her whole life looking after the family. That is how a eulogy is written, not a piece of writing.

## 1. The one move this lecture teaches

> **To write a person, first find one habit: something they do every time and other people don't.**

The test: **cover the name, and someone who knows them can still tell who it is.** "Kind" identifies no one. "At dinner, he always picks out the fish's eye for his grandson first" does.

## 2. How the masters do it

Lu Xun wrote Kong Yiji without saying he was poor, pedantic or proud. He wrote one action:

> 便排出九文大钱。

*Translation:* and laid out nine large coppers.

One verb, 排, "to lay out in a row." Others toss, fumble or hand over their money; he sets the coins on the counter one by one: poor, and insisting on dignity. Later, when he comes to drink with his legs broken, the money is "摸出四文大钱" (*Translation:* fished out four large coppers). From laying out to fishing out, a man has collapsed.

**A habit is evidence of character; a changed habit is evidence of fate.**

## 3. What AI writes

> My father was a man of few words who loved his family deeply; he gave silently in his own way and never said his love out loud.

"Few words", "loved deeply", "gave silently": three descriptions, not one action. Every father in the country fits.

## 4. One revision

**Original**
> My deskmate is a real stickler.

**Breakdown**: "stickler" is your judgment; the reader has seen no evidence.

**Revised**
> When my deskmate lends someone an eraser, he first writes the date in a notebook. When it comes back, he ticks it. Once, when I returned his eraser with a corner missing, he looked at it and drew a small triangle next to the tick.

It never says "stickler." Notebook, date, tick, small triangle: that is enough.

## 5. Your exercise

Write about someone you know well, in no more than 150 words. (The Chinese original sets 250 characters; 150 English words is roughly the same length.) **Three constraints:**

1. No word describing personality anywhere (kind, hardworking, fussy, gentle...).
2. Write only one habit, and show it happening twice.
3. The second time, let it change a little.

That small change is this person's story.
