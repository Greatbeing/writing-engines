# Lecture 1 · Opening: Don't Argue in the First Sentence

Start with six openings. The first three were written by a machine; the last three by people.

> In this fast-paced era, we often forget to stop and feel life.
>
> With the spread of social media, more and more young people are reexamining the value of solitude.
>
> Solitude is a skill, and also a kind of wisdom.

> 元丰六年十月十二日夜，解衣欲睡，月色入户，欣然起行。
> *Translation:* On the night of the twelfth day of the tenth month, in the sixth year of Yuanfeng, I had undressed for bed when moonlight came in through the door, and I got up gladly and went out. (Su Shi)
>
> 孔乙己是站着喝酒而穿长衫的唯一的人。
> *Translation:* Kong Yiji was the only customer who drank standing up and wore a long gown. (Lu Xun)
>
> 我家门前有两棵树，一株是枣树，还有一株也是枣树。
> *Translation:* In front of my house there are two trees. One is a jujube tree; the other is also a jujube tree. (Lu Xun)

The difference is not talent. **The machine gives the conclusion first; people give the event first.**

## 1. The one move this lecture teaches

> **Put something happening in the first sentence: a person, an action, an object. Push your point back, past the third sentence.**

There is one test: **read the first sentence on its own. Could it be a photograph?** If it could, it's right.

A sentence that fails: "Solitude is a skill." That isn't a photograph. It's a comment.

## 2. How the masters do it

In "A Night Visit to Chengtian Temple," Su Shi's first sentence doesn't say how lovely the moon was or how refined a night walk is. It gives a sequence of actions: the date, undressing for bed, moonlight entering the room, gladness, getting up and going out. **The conclusion "the moonlight was beautiful" is hidden inside "got up gladly and went out."**

When Lu Xun brings Kong Yiji on stage, the first sentence is an image so precise it puzzles: he drinks standing up, yet wears a long gown. No explanation. The explanation comes later.

In the famous opening of Lu Xun's "Autumn Night," the two trees get two clauses. Grammatically it's waste. As writing, it stops you from sliding past: **you have to stand in that yard and look at those two trees.**

What the three share: **each first sentence could be photographed.** Not one of them passes judgment.

## 3. What AI writes

The machine's habit is "summary first, examples after." It saw the claim-then-evidence structure so often in training that it puts the conclusion up front and wraps the whole piece in one big word.

"In this fast-paced era" carries no information. Its only job is to tell readers that the author is about to lecture, and then the readers leave.

"With the spread of…" is the same: a transition that takes responsibility for nothing.

**The test**: cover your first sentence and start reading from the second. If it still reads smoothly, the first sentence was filler.

## 4. One revision

**Original**
> In this fast-paced era, we often forget to stop and feel life.

**The fix**: delete "era" and replace it with a specific moment. Who, where, doing what.

**Revised**
> Eleven at night, the last train on the subway. The man across from me turned his phone face-down on his knee and kept it there until the end of the line.

The two are about the same length. Not one word of the original could go into a photograph; the revision has a specific person, a specific action, a specific time.

**Note**: nothing in the revision says "we forget to feel life." Readers will reach that conclusion on their own. **That is what handing the conclusion to the reader means.**

## 5. Your exercise

Write an opening of 40–70 words. (The Chinese original sets 60–100 characters; the word range is the English equivalent.) Any topic.

**Four constraints, all required:**

1. The words "era," "nowadays," "with the [rise/spread] of," and "we" may not appear. (These stand in for the Chinese 时代, 如今, 随着, 我们.)
2. The first sentence must contain a specific time or place.
3. The first sentence must contain an action (an action, not a state).
4. The first three sentences may not contain any evaluative word (beautiful, good, important, precious, rare, worthwhile).

**Self-check**: copy out your first sentence on its own and ask: **could this be shot as a photograph?** If it could only be shot as a caption, rewrite it.
