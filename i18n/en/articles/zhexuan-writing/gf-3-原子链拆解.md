## 2. Checking it against the combination rules

This chain is closest to "The People First": "Acknowledge → Reframe the Question → Anchoring with Data ×4 → Deconstruct to Enumerable → Concrete-Abstract Sandwich → Aphorism", which belongs to the crisis-guidance / hot-topic commentary register. The difference is in the order: the original puts **data first and emotion after**. Acknowledge gets only half a sentence, and the reader, defences still up, is handed a string of growth rates before anything else.

A different combination would be stronger: open with one specific person (a graduate job-hunting, say — the "12.7 million" in the original could perfectly well land on a single human being: that is a Scene Hook), then Acknowledge, then the data. The rhythm would shift from "let me tell you about it" to "let's do the sums together."

## 3. Red-line check

1. **Is the target specific**: broadly pass. Section 3 aims the guns at the concrete practice of "statistical releases spending their main length on total growth rates and structural highlights", without generalising to "the departments concerned". Section 2 names "the underlying logic of platform algorithms", which also lands on a concrete mechanism.
2. **Is the protective layer present**: present, and rather thick. Macro policy, economic fundamentals and international comparison all stand up; the passage on "external uncertainty" and "it has truly not been easy" is long, and the protective layer slightly overrides the firing position.
3. **Are the exits sufficient**: there are two exits — for the individual, "consciously step out of the cocoon"; for government departments, make statistical interpretation "more down-to-earth". The first is actionable; the second is more of a principle.
4. **Is the imagery unified**: "temperature gap" runs through the whole piece, and "transmission chain" and "triple filter" both serve it, with no mixing. This is the best thing the piece does.
5. **Number of aphorisms**: about three, within the ceiling. The strongest grows at an argumentative node in section 3: "Facing a problem does not manufacture pessimism; avoidance is what lets speculation and emotion fill the blank."
6. **Degree of lecturing**: the closing paragraph has "in the author's view" and "we might as well take a longer view"; the voice stays level, and "you should" never appears.

## 4. Conclusion

**Verdict in one line**: high precision in tearing a mechanism apart, solid data anchoring — a hot-topic commentary that holds up; the weak points are that Acknowledge comes too late and too short, and that the ending closes on persuasion rather than on a structured stance.

**The two things most worth changing**:

1. **Reorder the opening**: move "some people do feel a temperature gap" up to the first sentence, follow it with a Scene Hook — a specific job-seeker or small-business owner — and only then bring in the GDP figures. Anchoring with Data belongs where the doubt runs hottest ("on what grounds do you say the economy is fine"), not before the reader has spoken.
2. **Turn the ending into a three-line stance**: the present close is two paragraphs of advice plus one "in the author's view". It could be compressed into one set of three parallel lines — one to the government, one to the platforms, one to the individual — and the sentence already in the original, "it is not to force everyone to feel the same", can serve as the base of the third line.
