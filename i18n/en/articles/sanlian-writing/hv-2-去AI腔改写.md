# Rewrite: Busy for Whose Benefit

*Translated from the Chinese; the techniques are annotated where English cannot carry them.*

> The original is the “very AI” paragraph given in the prompt; the people and lines in the rewrite are invented for demonstration [to be verified].

Nine in the evening, and nobody on the team has left. Xiao Wang finished his work long ago, but he still has two documents open, switching back and forth. I ask him why he isn’t going. He says quietly, “The boss is still here. Leave early and it looks like you don’t care.”

Everyone is using overtime to prove they’re useful. Put plainly, the problem isn’t the people, it’s the appraisal: whoever leaves latest looks like they’ve done the most.
