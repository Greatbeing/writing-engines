> The user's words: Turn the meeting notes into minutes of an office meeting.

## The engine's reply

Minutes are not a transcript: they do not set down who said what; they distill the spirit of the meeting and the matters decided, each with its lead unit, cooperating units and deadline. The user supplied no notes, so everything below is an invented demonstration [to be verified]; when the real notes arrive, it will be rewritten in the same structure.

## Notes on delivery

- Minutes are generally not stamped with the official seal; "the meeting held" and "the meeting decided" introduce the content.
- The discussion and individual positions are not written at all; only the conclusions and the division of work are kept.
