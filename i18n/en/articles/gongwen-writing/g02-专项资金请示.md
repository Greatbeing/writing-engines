> The user's words: Our department has to apply to the provincial government for a sum of special-purpose funds. Write a request for instructions; I will give you the material afterwards.

## The engine's reply

The document type is right: to ask the government at your own level for approval, you use a **请示**, an upward document. A few points first:

- **One matter per document**: this request asks only for this one sum of special-purpose funds; other matters (establishment quotas, project approval) get requests of their own.
- **No invented figures**: your material has not arrived, so the amount, the basis for the estimate and the project list all stand as "××万元" [×× ten thousand yuan]. I will fill them in when the material comes; I do not write numbers out of thin air.
- **Signatory**: an upward document must carry the signatory in the letterhead block, normally the department's principal person in charge. Please have the secretariat mark it in red along with the rest.
- The request goes to the provincial government alone, with no copies to subordinate units.

## Notes on delivery

The grounds in this version are an empty frame. When the material arrives, put the weight on the "practical need" section — when the provincial government approves money, what it examines is whether the gap has been calculated solidly. On the scale of the funding, I would suggest checking the figures once more with the finance division.
