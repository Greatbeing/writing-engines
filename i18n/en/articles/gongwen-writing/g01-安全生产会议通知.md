# g01 Draft: Notice of a Work-Safety Conference

*Translated from the Chinese; the techniques are annotated where English cannot carry them.*

[Note: the document blocks keep the Chinese date order — 2026年11月25日 is 25 November 2026.]

> The user's words: Write me a notice — next Wednesday afternoon we're holding a work-safety conference for the whole system.

## The engine's reply (stage one: clarify the requirement; do not draft yet)

Good — I will write this meeting notice. Before I put pen to paper, let me confirm a few things with you, so that we do not have to do it twice:

1. **Issuing authority and addressees**: in whose name does it go out, and to whom? For instance, "××省××厅" to "the ×× bureaus of every city, county (district), all divisions of the department and its directly affiliated units" — or only within the department?
2. **Participants**: how many from each unit, and at what level? The principal person in charge, the person in charge of this area, or the section (division) chiefs for work safety? Is registration needed, and when is the reply slip due?
3. **Meeting particulars**: next Wednesday is 2 December. What time in the afternoon does it begin? Where? On site or by video (and how are the branch venues set up)? Roughly which items are on the agenda — is there a leader's speech, or a model-unit report for which material must be prepared in advance?

One more, off the cuff: is this a routine deployment, or has there been an accident lately that needs special emphasis? The two cases take a different weight of tone.

Tell me these, and I will draft it in one pass in the proper notice format.

## Notes on delivery

- No draft is produced until the time, the place and the scope of participants are confirmed; every element in the skeleton is a placeholder [to be verified].
- A meeting notice is a downward document: an explicit tone and complete particulars are enough; there is no need to write at length.
