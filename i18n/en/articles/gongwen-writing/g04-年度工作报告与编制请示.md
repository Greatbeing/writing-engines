> The user's words: Turn this year's work into a report, and while you are at it ask the higher level to approve more staff establishment for next year.

## The engine's reply

That "while you are at it" has to be taken apart: **a report must not carry a request for approval inside it**. A report reports on work; the higher level does not reply to it. If you ask for establishment approval inside the report, the higher level will most likely treat the document as one for reading, and the matter will not get done.

I suggest splitting it into two documents:

1. **"Report on the Work in 2026"** — work only, closing with "特此报告" (the above is hereby reported). The report may state objectively the problem that "personnel are insufficient", but it does not ask for approval.
2. **"Request for Instructions on Increasing the 2027 Personnel Establishment"** — a separate document, one matter per document, closing with "妥否，请批示" (whether appropriate, please instruct), sent to the establishment authority (please confirm the addressee [to be verified]).

Both are upward documents, and both must carry a signatory. Below is the first draft of the report; the request waits until you confirm the addressee and the number to be requested.
