## How the engine handles it

A polishing task takes the light path: read the draft through, check it against yourself, deliver — no follow-up questions, no research. The user's original text was an ellipsis, so a made-up but typical draft is used here for demonstration.

**Original (invented example)**

为了进一步切实有效地做好我们单位的安全生产各项工作，确保各项工作能够顺利地开展，经过单位领导班子研究讨论之后，决定在全单位范围内开展一次为期一个月时间的安全生产隐患排查整治专项行动，希望各个部门要高度重视起来，认真抓好落实。

Word for word: "In order to further practically and effectively do well the various tasks of work safety in our unit, to ensure that the various tasks can be carried out smoothly, after research and discussion by the unit's leadership team, it has been decided to carry out, throughout the whole unit, a special action for the investigation and rectification of work-safety hazards lasting a period of one month's time; it is hoped that every department will raise its attention high and earnestly grasp the implementation."

**After polishing**

为切实做好安全生产工作，经研究，决定在全单位开展为期一个月的安全隐患排查整治专项行动。请各部门高度重视，认真抓好落实。

Word for word: "To do the work of work safety well in real earnest, after study it has been decided to carry out across the whole unit a one-month special action to investigate and rectify safety hazards. All departments are asked to give it their full attention and to grasp the implementation earnestly."

## What was changed

- **Doubled modifiers cut**: of "further practically and effectively", only "practically" is kept; "one month's time" loses "time".
- **Empty words cut**: "to ensure that the various tasks can be carried out smoothly" repeats what came before; it is deleted.
- **Procedural wording compressed**: "after research and discussion by the unit's leadership team" becomes "after study".
- **"It is hoped that ... should" changed to "are asked to"**: a downward document states its requirement plainly; it does not use a consulting tone.
- The length drops from roughly 110 characters to roughly 60, and nothing is lost in meaning. If it is still too long, "All departments are asked to give it their full attention" can also go.
