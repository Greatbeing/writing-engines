> The user's words: The ×× commission, which is at our own level, has written asking for funds. We agree. Write a 批复.

## The engine's reply

I have to stop you here: **this cannot be a 批复; it has to be a 复函**.

A 批复 is used only to answer a 请示 from a **subordinate** body. The ×× Commission is a department at our own level, not subordinate to us. What they sent was a 函; what we send back must also be a 函 — a reply on a matter of approval uses a "复函", and it closes with "特此函复" (the above is hereby replied to). Using a 批复 would amount to treating them as a subordinate, which is awkward for them and, in terms of document type, a plain error.

I have drafted a reply letter for you. The amount and the document number of their incoming letter stand as placeholders until you verify them.
